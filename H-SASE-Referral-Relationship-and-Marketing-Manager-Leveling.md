# H-SASE : Referral Relationship and Marketing Manager Leveling

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Job Name** | Referral Relationship and Marketing Manager - Leveling |
| **Purpose** | Define the leveling for the referral & partner marketing function: building and nurturing referral and channel partner relationships, and designing and executing marketing programs that recruit, activate, and grow these partners to generate qualified pipeline and revenue for our enterprise-grade SASE products |
| **Main Responsibilities** | Build, nurture, and grow relationships with referral, affiliate, and channel partners<br>Design and execute partner-facing marketing programs (recruitment, onboarding, activation, and co-marketing campaigns)<br>Develop partner enablement assets, incentives, and referral mechanics that drive participation and conversion<br>Track, analyze, and report on referral channel performance (pipeline, conversion, attribution, ROI)<br>Interface with Sales, Product Marketing, Demand Generation, Legal, and Finance to align programs and operationalize referrals<br>Ensure partner experience, brand consistency, and compliance across all referral touchpoints |
| **Education / Qualifications** | Degree in Marketing, Business, Communications (Preferred) or practical relevant experience/knowledge<br>Very strong verbal and written English skills<br>Strong relationship-building, communication, and stakeholder-management skills<br>Comfort with marketing analytics, CRM, and partner/affiliate tooling |
