H-SASE : Referral Relationship and Marketing Manager Leveling

Job Name Referral Relationship and Marketing Manager - Leveling
Purpose Define the leveling for the referral & partner marketing function: building and nurturing referral and channel partner relationships, and designing and executing marketing programs that recruit, activate, and grow these partners to generate qualified pipeline and revenue for our enterprise-grade SASE products
Main Responsibilities
  • Build, nurture, and grow relationships with referral, affiliate, and channel partners
  • Design and execute partner-facing marketing programs (recruitment, onboarding, activation, and co-marketing campaigns)
  • Develop partner enablement assets, incentives, and referral mechanics that drive participation and conversion
  • Track, analyze, and report on referral channel performance (pipeline, conversion, attribution, ROI)
  • Interface with Sales, Product Marketing, Demand Generation, Legal, and Finance to align programs and operationalize referrals
  • Ensure partner experience, brand consistency, and compliance across all referral touchpoints
Education / Qualifications
  • Degree in Marketing, Business, Communications (Preferred) or practical relevant experience/knowledge
  • Very strong verbal and written English skills
  • Strong relationship-building, communication, and stakeholder-management skills
  • Comfort with marketing analytics, CRM, and partner/affiliate tooling
