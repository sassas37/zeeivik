Mission Statment

We don't simply perform surgery. We create the best surgical experience for every pet, every family, and every veterinarian we serve through exceptional care, transparent communication, and complete recovery support.

2

Reqruitment of overnight Nurse

Recruit an overnight nurse to strengthen ICU coverage, support post-operative monitoring, improve continuity of patient care, and reduce workload pressure on the daytime team.

2- high

  • Define required overnight nurse qualifications, ICU skills, and schedule expectations

  • Publish recruitment ad for overnight nursing coverage

  • Screen candidates for emergency/ICU experience and ability to work independently overnight

  • Create onboarding plan, training checklist, and shadow shifts with senior staff

  • Confirm compensation, shift differential, and retention plan

2026-Q3

To Do

Supports VSS strength in overnight ICU care and addresses SWOT workforce challenges: limited support staff, insufficient training, and recruitment process gaps.

3

Complete purchase CT scan

Purchase and implement a CT scanner to expand diagnostic capability, reduce dependency on outside imaging, and support surgical planning and business growth.

2- high

  • Finalize CT scanner requirements, vendor options, and budget

  • Confirm financing, room preparation, installation needs, and service contract

  • Create training plan for doctors and support staff

  • Define launch timeline, pricing, and referral communication plan

2026-Q4

To Do

Based on VSS SWOT weakness: no CT scanner available.
