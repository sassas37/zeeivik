The Situation
A clinically excellent practice in a structurally invisible body.
Dr. Zeev Schwartz holds a rare triple credential — DACVS-SA + CCRT + CVAT — that no benchmark competitor matches in one person. Yet his name and board certification do not appear above the fold, and the practice runs as a single-surgeon shop in the most contested referral corridor on the East Coast.

The Contrarian Call
The CT
Scanner
Verdict: NO · deferred to Year 2
REASON 1
Wrong sequencing — technology without a second surgeon to use it is not a referral driver. At a single-surgeon practice it produces zero incremental volume.
REASON 2
Wrong technology — the in-budget cone-beam unit is clinically inferior for ortho/soft-tissue planning; a proper helical CT runs $300–500K+.
REASON 3
Weak differentiator here — PASE (across the river) and NorthStar already have on-site CT/MRI plus multiple surgeons and decades of relationships.
REASON 4
No in-year break-even — at ~$800/scan and 2–3 scans/week against $200–300K+ installed, payback runs 24–30+ months.
RECOMMENDATION
Lease / send-out if CT is needed for workup. Revisit ownership as a Year-2 decision, gated on proven utilization and the second surgeon being seated.
