The Starting Point
A Scarce Asset in a Fragile Envelope
🏆

The Asset

A rare ACVS-SA surgeon — also CCRT & CVAT. Surgery → rehab → pain under one clinician.

⚠️

The Fragility

100% of revenue rides on one surgeon. No ER / after-hours. No in-house CT/MRI.

📉

The Commercial Gap

No published pricing, thin digital presence, and the strongest differentiator sits unused.

🏟️

The Arena

Corporates dominate specialty; even the nearest independent is full-service 24/7. We must win on focus.

"Zero" isn't a failing practice — it's a great surgeon and team trapped below their potential. The gaps are commercial and structural, not foundational.
The Strategy
Don't out-scale the corporates.
Out-focus them.
Become the region's trusted independent surgical-recovery destination — chosen by GPs and pet families on three proof-backed pillars.

Continuity

The surgeon you meet is the surgeon who operates and follows recovery — impossible at rotating-staff corporates.

Access

Consults in days, not weeks — and a direct line to the surgeon, while large centers run 1hr+ waits.

Honesty

Transparent indicative pricing and no upsell — answering the documented corporate-pricing backlash.

Strategic Assessment
SWOT
Strengths
  • Scarce triple-credentialed ACVS surgeon (surgery + rehab + acupuncture)
  • Independence — pricing freedom amid corporate backlash
  • Surgeon continuity + direct GP access
  • Referral coordinator already in seat; CareCredit / Trupanion rails
Weaknesses
  • Single-surgeon key-person risk (100% revenue)
  • No ER / after-hours; no in-house CT/MRI
  • Thin digital; no published pricing
  • Strongest differentiator (transparency) unused; form-only GP program
Opportunities
  • Aging-pet / orthopedic demand wave
  • Pricing-transparency vacuum + corporate backlash
  • Pet-insurance growth lifts surgical approvals
  • Rehab / regenerative / pain boom; independent-surgeon talent preference
Threats
  • 2024–26 cyclical downturn
  • Corporate scale + ER funnels (BluePearl, RBVH, Saint Francis)
  • Capable independent neighbors (Mount Laurel, NorthStar)
  • Surgeon scarcity makes hiring #2 hard; single-complication risk w/o ER
